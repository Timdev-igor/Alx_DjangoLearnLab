About Me

Photo

Second-year Computer Science student at Karatina University and backend developer in the ALX Africa Back-End Development Program, working toward an internship and junior/mid-level backend role. Core stack is Python, Django, and Django REST Framework, with hands-on cloud infrastructure experience.

Recent work includes building a full e-commerce API with JWT authentication, atomic checkout, and rate-limited endpoints, adding Celery for async task processing, and optimizing performance with caching and database indexing. Currently working through deployment and DevOps — splitting settings for different environments and shipping to Microsoft Azure.

Outside of backend work, runs a creative brand called Opas TV and is building an original fictional universe called THRENODY, with interests spanning filmmaking, fashion, and fitness alongside the engineering side.

Documents the process in public — the wins, the debugging sessions, and everything in between — as part of building toward a recognizable body of work.
